Welcome to Pat's Educare Centre

Since 2011, Pat's Educare Centre has been a safe, caring and stimulating place for children aged 0 to 5 to play, learn and grow. Our Montessori-integrated programme is child-centred and hands-on, and our small classes mean every child gets the attention they deserve.

Parents can relax knowing their little ones are in secure, CCTV-monitored classrooms, enjoying two nutritional meals a day, big creative playgrounds and fun on-site activities such as swimming, martial arts, speech and drama and Actionball.
