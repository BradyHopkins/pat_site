Feedback and Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the enrolment fees?

For the 2026 academic year, we offer two options for children aged 1-5 years:

  • Option 1: R2300 per month - includes 2 school meals daily.
  • Option 2: R2100 per month - includes school breakfast (parents supply lunch and snacks).

Both options include care and educational stimulation following the NCF and Montessori curriculum, toys, equipment and 1 educational excursion. See our full Fee Structure on the Services page for more detail.

Till what time is the educare open?

Monday - Thursday: 6:30 AM - 4:30 PM.

Friday: 6:30 AM - 3:00 PM.

Aftercare is available at an additional cost. We are open during school holidays (except December).

We are closed on public and special holidays.

What ages do you take?

We care for and teach children aged 1 to 5 years.

Which meals are provided?

Option 1 includes two school meals daily. Option 2 includes school breakfast, and parents supply lunch and snacks. Daily snacks at 10:00 (fruit, yogurt, water) and 14:00 (sandwich, juice, chips or mini cheddar biscuits) are part of our schedule.

Do you offer aftercare, transport and extramurals?

Yes. Aftercare and scholar transport are available at an additional cost, and children can take part in on-site swimming, martial arts, speech and drama and Actionball.

Are you open during school holidays?

We are open during school holidays except December, and closed on public and special holidays.

How is my child kept safe?

We use CCTV cameras, controlled classrooms and secure play areas, and keep classes small so educators know every child well.
