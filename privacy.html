Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with Pat's Educare Centre.

Information We Collect

We collect the contact and account details you provide through our contact form or when signing up for an account (such as name, email address and any message you send us), along with basic enrolment information for registered families.

How We Use It

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, manage enrolment, and communicate important updates about the centre. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

You can ask us to update or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us. Account and authentication data is securely managed through our Supabase-hosted database.

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out via the Contact page.
